### Project Description
Who doesn't like a bit of tomfoolery? We present to you our chrome extension MemeMaxx, which replaces ads with memes. Moreover, the user can upload their own custom images to pull a prank on their friends or to personalize the memes torment the user in his beaautiful way 😍

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
The internet is overrun by boring intrusive ads that drains the soul of people who browse the internet.
### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We replace the intrusive ads with even more intrusive memes. Since the internet was unusable anyway, might as well have a giggle :)
